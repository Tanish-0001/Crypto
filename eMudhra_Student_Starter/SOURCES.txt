Primary sources reviewed 9 November 2026
=======================================

[1] CERT-In, CIAD-2026-0050, Compromise of eMudhra Intermediate CA
Certificates, 21 October 2026.
https://cert-in.org.in/s2cMainServlet?VLCODE=CIAD-2026-0050&pageid=PUBVLNOTES02
Supports: affected P-384 scalars below 256; distinction from the Indian DSC/eSign
infrastructure; key-generation weakness rather than a break of the curve.

[2] eMudhra, Statement on Recent Certificate Security Incident.
First published 20 October 2026; page last updated 5 November 2026.
https://emudhra.com/en/post/certificate-security-incident/
Supports: vendor account of key wrap/unwrap and script failures; vendor statements
about separated infrastructure. These are attributed claims, not independently
verified implementation details. The laboratory is not a reconstruction of its script.

[3] Mozilla Security Team, Protecting Firefox users following the compromise
of eMudhra intermediate certificates, 20 October 2026; update 21 October.
https://blog.mozilla.org/security/2026/10/20/protecting-firefox-users-following-the-compromise-of-emudhra-intermediate-certificates/
Supports: 17 October report, emergency OneCRL distribution and root-store response.

[4] Cloudflare, 4 real? Tracking exploitation and patching of eMudhra's weak
keys, 30 October 2026.
https://blog.cloudflare.com/emudhra-patching/
Supports: private scalar 4 as one example; forged certificates observed after
disclosure, without evidence of interception of Cloudflare's connections in
that report; role of alternate certification paths and varying client policies.

[5] NIST SP 800-186, Recommendations for Discrete Logarithm-based
Cryptography: Elliptic Curve Domain Parameters, February 2023.
https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/186/final
P-384 parameters in curve_params.py; the implementation is independently checked
against the cryptography library rather than trusting copied parameters alone.

[6] RFC 5280, Internet X.509 Public Key Infrastructure Certificate and
Certificate Revocation List (CRL) Profile, May 2008, especially sections 4.2 and 6.
https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5280
The full standard has additional requirements and updates. The lab implements
only its explicitly stated educational profile.

[7] PyCA cryptography X.509 and elliptic-curve API documentation.
https://cryptography.io/en/latest/x509/reference/
https://cryptography.io/en/latest/hazmat/primitives/asymmetric/ec/
The supplied code was tested against version 50.0.0.

[8] NIST FIPS 186-5, Digital Signature Standard, February 2023.
https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/186-5/final
ECDSA signing and verification; the student arithmetic is instructional and
is not constant-time or suitable for production signing.
